#10 From Awareness to Action: Rethinking Climate Visual Communication

Author(s): Elena Drakulevska

Speaker Biographies

Elena Drakulevska

Principal Product Manager at Microsoft working on Power BI Core Visuals, where I help shape the future of reporting and data visualization. I’m an international speaker, Microsoft MVP alumna, and founder of MoonStory, a platform focused on UX Design and Data Viz. Through conferences, mentoring, and community initiatives, I share my mission of making technology more human, empowering and joyful

Climate data visualisation has produced some of the most striking and visually compelling work of our time. Yet many climate visuals, while widely admired, struggle when they are asked to support deeper understanding, comparison, or decision-making.

In this talk, I explore the tension between aesthetic impact and usability through a UX and visual communication lens, not as a critique of artistic or scientific climate visualisation, but as a reflection on intent. By introducing the idea of different “design modes” in climate communication, awareness, understanding, and action, I suggest that many challenges arise not from poor design, but from mismatched expectations about what a visual is meant to do.

The talk invites designers, scientists, journalists, and communicators to reflect on what we ask our climate visualisations to support, who they are designed to serve, and how greater clarity of purpose can help climate data move from admiration toward meaningful understanding and action.

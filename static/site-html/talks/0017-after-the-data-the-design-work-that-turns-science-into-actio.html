#28 The Last Mile: The Design Work That Transforms Science Into Action

Author(s): Stephen Downs, Alex Safchuk

Speaker Biographies

Alexander Safchuk

Alex spent a decade leading Experience Design at Huge Inc for clients like Google, Brooks, and Epic Games. Joining Ode in 2023 as an Experience Design Partner, he now applies his commercial expertise to build digital products and tell captivating stories, driving positive impact for both people and the planet.

Environmental data has never been more abundant. Satellites track methane plumes in real time. Ocean sensors follow whales across thousands of miles. Coastal models price climate risk down to the parcel level. The science is extraordinary, yet most of it is sitting in a database somewhere, doing nothing.

The problem isn't the data. It's the last mile.

The last mile is where design transforms scientific knowledge into tangible stories that in the hands of the right people, can make real change. Drawing on three projects: Carbon Mapper's methane emissions portal, the Blue Corridors whale migration tracker, and ORRAA’s Coastal Risk Index protecting over $363 billion in assets, we'll show what it looks like when design changes the conversation and speaks information into action.

#1 Designing for Climate Action: From Data to Decisions

Author(s): Cristina Jaramago Bertrand, Elena Palao, Karl Scheifinger, Pino Mussak

Speaker Biographies

Cristina Jaramago Bertrand

Cristina Jaramago Bertrand is a skilled visual designer with a passion for creating impactful digital experiences. Cristina's expertise spans art direction, interactive and prototype design, UI design, and the development of scalable digital products. Her ability to solve complex problems and craft intuitive, visually compelling solutions defines her work.

Elena Palao

Elena Palao is a Senior Data Scientist at Vizzuality. Her work connects geospatial analysis, environmental science, and the frameworks organisations use to act on nature and climate risk. She focuses on turning complex spatial data into tools that support real decisions, and on making the analysis behind them rigorous enough to trust and clear enough to use.

Karl Scheifinger

Karl Scheifinger is a researcher in the Integrated Assessment and Climate Change Research Group of the IIASA Energy, Climate, and Environment Program. Recently, he worked on the Second Austrian Assessment Report on Climate Change and a comprehensive assessment of Austrian climate policies. Currently, Karl is coordinating the Scenario Compass Initiative.

Pino Mussak

Pino Mussak is a research software engineer in the Scenario Services and Scientific Software team of the IIASA Energy Climate and Environment Program. His responsibilities include the development of tools for dissemination of scientific research results, including user front ends and the required database backend infrastructure. He holds a bachelor degree in Computer Science from the Vienna Uni.

Vizzuality is submitting a workshop proposal in collaboration with the International Institute for Applied Systems Analysis (IIASA). Through this contribution, we aim to enrich the conversation and share our joint perspective on applied science and data visualization in the context of climate science and environmental impact. The goal is to share Vizzuality’s experience designing tools that help people better understand—and make decisions about—climate and nature systems, as well as to bring forward our work with IIASA on the Scenario Compass Initiative, a platform designed to facilitate navigation of climate future scenarios using the most up-to-date data-driven models.

This hands-on workshop presents Vizzuality’s approach to designing digital tools that support the interpretation and decision-making of climate and environmental systems, using as a reference one of the platforms developed in collaboration with the International Institute for Applied Systems Analysis (IIASA), the Scenario Compass Initiative Platform.

Participants will explore how large complex data related to climate drivers can be transformed into decision-support interfaces and learn practical design techniques for communicating uncertainty, complexity, and long-term environmental change.

1.What makes a good climate decision tool? (15 min)

2. Framing the challenge (10 min)

3. Hands-on design activity (40 min)

4. Rapid sharing and critique (20 min)

5. Reflection and takeaways (5 min)

Workshop outcomes

Participants will:

* Learn practical techniques for climate data visualisation

* Explore design patterns for decision-support tools

* Understand how to communicate uncertainty effectively

* Learn how to understand user needs and link scientific outputs to those needs

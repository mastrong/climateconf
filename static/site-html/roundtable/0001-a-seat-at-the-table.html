A Seat at the Table: Information Designers Inside Scientific Institutions

Panelists: Claire Ransom (WMO), Dr Anna Lombardi (ECMWF/Copernicus), WOO Qiyun (IPCC), Dr Freya Muir (ESA)
Chair: Tom Gabriel Johansen (InfoDesignLab)
Introduction: Anna Pirani (CMCC)
Sponsored by

Major scientific institutions have begun employing information designers and data visualisation experts, laying a foundation for a more professional field. Practitioners from WMO, ECMWF/Copernicus, IPCC and ESA discuss what this shift looks and feels like today: new recognition and opportunities, alongside vulnerability, pressure and the growing impact of AI. Having earned a seat at the table, how does the profession keep developing, and where is it going?

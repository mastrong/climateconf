#26 Design: The Catalyst for Climate Action

Author(s): Alexander Safchuk

Speaker Biographies

Alexander Safchuk

Alex spent a decade leading Experience Design at Huge Inc for clients like Google, Brooks, and Epic Games. Joining Ode in 2023 as an Experience Design Partner, he now applies his commercial expertise to build digital products and tell captivating stories, driving positive impact for both people and the planet.

Environmental data has never been more abundant. Satellites track methane plumes in real time. Ocean sensors follow whales across thousands of miles. Coastal models price climate risk down to the parcel level. The science is extraordinary, yet most of it sits on hard drives and in research papers.

When tackling our biggest climate challenges, the problem isn't the data—it’s what to do with it.

Design is the vital translation layer between raw science and human action. From foundational user experience to final interaction details, design determines whether data stays buried or becomes an engine for policy, investment, and conservation. Drawing on three projects—Carbon Mapper's methane emissions portal, the Blue Corridors whale migration tracker, and ORRAA’s Coastal Risk Index protecting over $363 billion in assets—we’ll show how intentional design throughout the digital build turns passive information into real-world change.
